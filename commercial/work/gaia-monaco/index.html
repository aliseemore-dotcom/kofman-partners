What we do

Real estate, for restaurants and retail. Nothing else.

We source prime sites and negotiate the deals that put the world's best F&B and retail brands into them. We are not lawyers, accountants or financiers — we are the real estate advisors who know exactly which ones you need, and bring them to the table.

The address is the product

The seat we take

One mandate, one seat — stated up front on every engagement.
01

For brands & tenants

You know where you want to be; we know how to get you there. We identify the right location, negotiate the terms, and structure the deal — then surround it with the professionals a market entry needs.

02

For landlords & developers

A prime site deserves a covenant worth building for. We bring landlords the brands that hold an address for fifteen years, not five — and negotiate tenancies both sides can live with.

03

For brands entering new markets

Franchise structures, partnerships, the right local vehicle — we place brands into markets they couldn't enter alone.

The deal, end to end

One

Identify the location

The right address for the brand — sourced through landlord relationships, mostly before sites reach the market.

Two

Negotiate the deal

Every term with the landlord, structured and closed.

Three

Assemble the professionals

Accountants, lawyers, employment and licensing specialists, designers, contractors — we are none of these, and we know exactly who the best of each are. They come with us.

Four

Source the financing

Introductions to the capital that funds fit-outs and entries.

Five

Stand up the framework

Operators, employment structures, the legal scaffolding — connected, not performed.

The network

Eighteen years of transactions build a bench — the landlords and estates of London and Dubai, the region's developers, and the advisors who've closed these deals with us before.

The network is the product as much as the address is.

The owners' note

We also open and operate restaurants of our own — MNKY HSE, African Queen and SANA among them, all tagged Founder Ventures on this site. We keep that side of the business separate from client mandates, and disclosed. But it's why our judgement on a site is an owner's, not a broker's.
